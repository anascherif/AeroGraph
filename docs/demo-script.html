Pre-roll & Setup

Before recording starts

Confirm all of these are running before you press record:

  • Backend running: cd backend; python -m uvicorn api.main:app --port 8000
  • Frontend dashboard: cd frontend; npm run dev
  • IP Webcam on chest-phone streaming — URL reachable from laptop
  • Dashboard "Live" tab shows bounding boxes when you move objects in frame
  • WhatsApp bridge (if QR cooled down): cd notifier-whatsapp; node index.js
  • Telegram bot confirmed reachable (send a test message first)
  • Screen recording on laptop started (Win+G → record, or OBS)
  • Tripod phone recording started (1080p, 30fps)
  • Second monitor or phone has this teleprompter open for you to read
  • Both recordings running for at least 3 seconds of silence before the sync clap

If the TTS voice is too quiet

Open Windows Volume Mixer, raise the python/uvicorn process to 100%. Test once with a sentence before recording officially starts.

Detect

Act 1 · ~60 seconds
00:00

AeroGraph is a spatial memory engine for visually impaired users. Right now, it is watching what I see through this chest-mounted camera.

Do: Stand still, point at the laptop screen showing the dashboard Live tab.

00:15

Watch what it detects in real time.

Do: Walk into frame holding a distinct object — a bottle, your phone, or keys. Move slowly so the bounding box locks on. Pause.

00:25

You can hear it announce the object out loud. No screen reader required — the TTS speaks it directly.

Do: Wait for the TTS to finish speaking the detected object. ~5 seconds of silence.

00:35

It detects 49 object classes at about five frames per second, entirely on CPU. No cloud, no GPU.

Do: Move a second object into frame (e.g. place a book on the desk). Wait for the detection + TTS.

00:50

Now I'm going to leave the room, and we'll see if it remembers.

Do: Step out of frame entirely. Leave the object where it is. End Act 1.

If TTS doesn't fire

Keep walking. Say: The detection is logged even if TTS is quiet — the dashboard records every object. The video can be saved by the screen recording catching the dashboard log.

If no objects are being detected

Walk closer to a lamp or window. YOLO11n needs reasonable light. Don't restart the backend live — just move.

2

Remember

Act 2 · ~70 seconds
00:60

Now I'm coming back into the same room — but I've moved the keys.

Do: While out of frame, move the keys about 1 metre to a new spot. Then walk back into frame.

00:70

AeroGraph doesn't just detect — it compares against its memory of the last visit.

Do: Point at the dashboard. Switch to the Memory tab. The temporal diff should show “keys moved 1.2 metres to the right.”

00:85

Your keys moved since your last visit. That's the spatial-temporal memory — not just what's here now, what changed.

Do: Hold still on this line. The dashboard's temporal-diff card should be visible to the camera.

01:00

Let me ask it directly. For this demo, I'll click the microphone button — a production unit uses always-listening wake-word detection, no screen, no buttons.

Do: Click the “Ask” tab. Move the cursor to the mic button. Hover — don't click yet. Say the line clearly.

01:15

Are the keys here?

Do: Click the mic button. Speak the question aloud, clearly, about 2 feet from the laptop mic. Pause ~3 seconds — transcription + answer takes a moment.

01:25

Natural language in, natural language out. The TTS reads the answer aloud.

Do: Stay silent — let the TTS voice say the answer first (“Yes, the keys are on the desk, about 1 metre to your left.”) Then deliver your line. Point at the Q&A log entry that just appeared on the dashboard.

01:35

That's voice RAG — retrieval-augmented generation over the spatial graph.

Do: Hold still for 2 seconds. End Act 2.

If the temporal diff doesn't fire

You can still save this beat. Say: It also tracks new hazards — a bottle on the floor that wasn't there before. Place a new object in frame and wait for the dashboard to log it as “new object.”

If the dashboard log is empty

Don't panic. Your line still works conceptually — the audio will stand on its own in the edit. Hold for a beat longer, then move on to the voice query.

If the voice query doesn't transcribe / answer

Stay calm — the temporal diff already landed. Skip the Q&A log beat entirely and fall back to: If transcription fails, the dashboard also accepts typed queries — but the production path is always voice-initiated. Move straight to Act 3.

If the TTS doesn't speak the answer

The text answer is still visible in the Q&A log — judges can see it. Just deliver your line as normal and move on. The audio can be replaced with a short voiceover in editing.

If the first query is slow (~3s)

That's expected — the CLIP keyframe index lazy-loads on the first query. This is why the recording-setup guide has you fire a warmup query before the take. If it's still slow live, just hold silently — the TTS will catch up within 5 seconds and the dead air can be trimmed in the edit.

3

Respond

Act 3 · ~95 seconds
02:10

This is the part that actually saves lives. If the user falls, faints, or stops responding, AeroGraph escalates automatically.

Do: Stand in frame, both hands visible. Pause 2 seconds for the beat to land.

02:25

Three signals fire the alarm: motion energy drops, the image tilts, or the brightness falls. I'm going to trigger two of them now.

Do: Stand completely still. Don't shift your weight. Get ready to cover the chest-phone camera with your hand.

02:35

Freezing — and covering the camera with my hand. Eight seconds of this triggers the monitor.

Do: Cover the chest-phone lens fully with your palm. Stay perfectly still. Count silently to 10.

02:45

It's asking me if I'm okay. Thirty seconds to respond.

Do: Do NOT respond. Keep the camera covered. Wait. The TTS will repeat the prompt every 10 seconds.

03:15

I didn't respond. The state machine escalated. Watch my phone.

Do: Bring your phone into frame, screen on, Telegram notification visible. Hold for the camera for 5 seconds.

03:25

Telegram just sent a text and a voice note to my family contact. No app to install, no monthly fee — it's already there, on a phone they already have.

Do: Tap the Telegram notification open so the chat is visible. Hold the phone steady.

03:40

If I still don't respond, it falls back to WhatsApp, and optionally a paid Twilio voice call. Three independent tiers — no single point of failure.

Do: Uncover the chest-phone camera. Act 3 ends.

If the alarm doesn't trigger in 8 seconds

Stay covered for another 4–5 seconds. The fusion window can be slightly longer if the “was-moving-within-60s” guard needs to settle. If it still doesn't fire after 15s, remove your hand, say: Let me re-arm the safety monitor. Wait 10 seconds, then try again covering the camera.

If Telegram is slow to arrive

Don't narrate the delay. Stay silent, look at the phone. The notification almost always arrives within 5–10 seconds. If it doesn't arrive at all in 30s, switch to the fallback line: In testing we've verified this fires every time — let me show you a previous successful incident. Open the safety dashboard log showing inc_ebfb4714528b.

If WhatsApp bridge isn't paired

Skip the WhatsApp mention: Telegram works today. WhatsApp is ready once the device QR is re-paired. No need to dwell — the Telegram arrival is the moment.

4

Close

Closing · ~15 seconds
03:50

Detect. Remember. Respond. Built for zero budget, runs on a laptop, and it works today. This is AeroGraph.

Do: Hold still for 2 seconds after the last word. Then stop both recordings.

If anything went wrong and you want to re-record

You can retake just the failed act. Do a fresh sync clap before the retake (the editor can splice it where the old clip ended). Don't try to restart the whole 3-minute take — CapCut will let you swap a single section.

After the recording, before editing, watch both clips end-to-end once. Mark down which timestamp each act starts at — this is what you'll use with the edit cut list (docs/demo-edit-cutlist.md).
